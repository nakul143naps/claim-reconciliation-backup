1. Executive Summary & Problem Solved

In healthcare provider billing, clinics submit charges (Claims) to health insurers and receive back complex electronic payment advices (Remittances). These remittances bundle cash disbursements, contractual discounts, patient copays, and clinical denial codes.

Standard enterprise billing systems often lose financial integrity because:

  • Floating-point arithmetic drift (e.g. 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004) creates auditing discrepancies.
  • Orphaned payments (checks received for patient accounts not yet in the billing system) are either discarded (losing cash accountability) or tied to fake dummy accounts (falsely inflating Accounts Receivable).
  • Operational denials vs. mathematical balances: Claims rejected under authorization codes (e.g. CO-197) that are later resolved and paid with contractual write-offs (CO-45) lose their audit trail.

This platform is a production-grade reconciliation engine and interactive audit hub that deterministically balances every transaction, tracks prior denial friction, isolates orphan cash, and provides instant batch CSV ingestion.
